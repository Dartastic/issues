# Code of Conduct

This community follows the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## TL;DR

- Be kind. Be specific. Be patient.
- Disagree on ideas, not on people.
- Don't post anything you wouldn't say in front of the whole room.
- Spam, harassment, off-topic content, and bad-faith argument get
  hidden — repeat offenders get blocked.

## Reporting

If you see something that crosses the line, email
[support@dartastic.io](mailto:support@dartastic.io). Reports stay
confidential. We'll act within a business day.
